Team Tyler

A chief of staff and a roster of role-based agents run the side projects. Written rules, one decision log, approval gates, and a usage budget. I am the final approver.

The problem

Several agents with access to the same projects will duplicate work, repeat a stale fact, and spend the budget unless someone assigns roles and writes down what they may not do. This is the governance layer for the other projects on this site.

How it works

Amber is chief of staff. She owns the priority list, routes the work, and brings me a decision as one card with the recommended option first. I keep at most three ranked priorities. Reversible, free actions she can decide. Spending, sending as me, deleting, publishing, trading, hiring, and legal calls wait for me.

A freeze stays on until I lift it in my own words. A relay of that lift counts only when my words are quoted verbatim, with the time and the place, the specific thing being lifted is named, and the chief of staff is the one relaying it.

One agent places orders. No other agent can change an order. When two sources disagree, both stay, and the conflict is flagged.

A decision I changed

Bot-to-bot chat was 44% of a week's usage. I measured that before I wrote the rule. Bot-to-bot messaging became opt-in, group rooms became announce-only, and the target moved to 60% of usage facing me. Asking the agents to talk less, without changing the routes, had already failed.
