BarBuildr

A mobile app that helps someone build a home whiskey bar. It tracks the bottles on your shelf and recommends the next one to buy, based on what your collection is missing.

The problem

People new to whiskey don't know what to buy next. Most advice is a ranked list of "best bottles," which ignores what you already own. Three similar bourbons make a worse bar than three different styles.

The recommendation engine

The core of the app is a gap engine. It doesn't rank bottles. It looks at your shelf and finds what's missing in three ways.

  1. Your shelfThe bottles you own
  2. Flavor gapSweet, spice, fruity, smoky or rich
  3. Cocktail gapA classic you can't make yet
  4. Proof gapNothing easy-drinking, or nothing bold
  5. Up to 3 picksEach with a plain reason
  • Flavor. Every bottle carries flavor tags grouped into five families. If your shelf has no smoky bottle, the engine suggests the most beginner-friendly one.
  • Cocktails. It checks six classics, such as the Manhattan and the Old Fashioned, and suggests the most versatile bottle for the first one you can't make.
  • Proof. It checks whether you have something under 43% ABV for guests and something at 50% or above for cocktails.

Each recommendation comes with a reason in plain words, so the app teaches as it suggests. It's rules, not machine learning, which keeps every suggestion explainable.

How it's built

The app is React Native with Expo and TypeScript, across 15 screens. Supabase provides the Postgres database and user sign-in, and the bottle catalog is defined in SQL. Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure storage.

Status

BarBuildr is a working prototype that runs on a phone through Expo. It hasn't been released to the app stores.
