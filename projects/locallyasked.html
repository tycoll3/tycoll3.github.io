Personal project · paused Oct 28, 2026

LocallyAsked

A short test of whether a local home-service business would pay for a monthly report on how AI assistants answer questions about their trade. I paused it. The findings that did not reproduce stay in the record.

The problem

A plumber, an HVAC shop, or a roofer can be named, misnamed, or left out when someone asks an AI. The question I actually needed answered was whether an owner would pay monthly for a report and for written fixes. I wanted a real offer, tested cheaply, before any more building.

How it works

The offer, written down and never launched: a monthly report on 20 fixed local questions, with the errors first and a score second. The price was $99 a month, cancel anytime, with no refund for the current month. LocallyAsked is the working name. I had not picked a final one.

  1. 20 questionsFixed, and reused exactly
  2. CaptureAI surfaces, one stored row each
  3. One-row ruleA finding has to rebuild from that row
  4. VerdictTest the offer before building more

Two method rules sat next to the one-row rule. I don't compare captures that used different location pins. A synthesized finding does not get labeled as a specific consumer app.

What I actually ran

What I withdrew

These were written up, then pulled, because a second look could not rebuild them or they did not reproduce. The wrong lines stay in the notes next to the correction.

  • A brunch question where two answer surfaces named no businesses in common.
  • A citation whose source was not in the stored row.
  • A claim that "the surface comes and goes."
  • A measured rate for how long an answer stayed put.
  • A line that said the study measured ChatGPT. On the machine I used, that surface answered 2 of 15.

Three findings survived the one-row rule. One stored row showed two different phone numbers for the same shop. One shop's review count mixed two platforms. One question's answers moved across repeats.

The stress test came back TEST FIRST. The fixes were the product. A dashboard on its own was not worth $99.

This was a validation test. The offer was never launched. It has no customers. I contacted no one.

A decision I changed

I paused an earlier $49 one-time offer on Oct 20, then wrote the $99 monthly offer, then paused that too on Oct 28. The files stay. The offer was never launched.

The harder change was the findings. I had treated the five claims above as results. When they failed the one-row rule, I took them out of the conclusion and left the retraction visible. A clean story would have been the easier edit.

Status

Paused on Oct 28, 2026. The evidence file is there for whoever picks it up, including me. Nothing is for sale.

Open questions

The untested fact is whether anyone says yes to $99 and pays again the next month. Outreach stayed at zero on purpose, until the offer was final and I lifted the hold. The name is still unsettled.
